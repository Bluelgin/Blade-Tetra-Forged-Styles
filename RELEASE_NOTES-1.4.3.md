# Blade Tetra: Forged Styles 1.4.3

The other awakened blades noticed Akatsuki was getting all the attention.

- **Senbonzakura** now gathers petals faster and turns them into a much fuller phantom-sword bloom.
- **Raikiri** gained reliable chain conduction, stronger wet-weather reach and an SA overload discharge.
- **Kyouka Suigetsu** now records Iaido cuts, reflects them on the next draw and shatters the reflection with **Water Moon Break**.
- Added new mirror-pool, shattered-glass and lightning effects while keeping SlashBlade's own slash visuals where they already work best.

Short version: more petals, more lightning, and the mirror now fights back.
