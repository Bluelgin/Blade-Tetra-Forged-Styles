## Mikage / Wine Fox-derived artwork

The following bundled resources are adaptations, not MIT-licensed artwork:

- `assets/blade_tetra/models/entity/mikage_tailored.json`
- `assets/blade_tetra/textures/entity/mikage_tailored.png`

Original character: Wine Fox / 酒狐, by TartaricAcid / 酒石酸菌.
Model and texture: Touhou Little Maid model-pack contributors
(original pack author field: "Very Many Authors").
Based on the local Akatsuki Taisho Wine Fox 1.2.3 adaptation by Taki
and Blade Tetra contributors.

Original project: https://github.com/TartaricAcid/TouhouLittleMaid

Source model:
https://github.com/TartaricAcid/TouhouLittleMaid/blob/1.20/src/main/resources/assets/touhou_little_maid/tlm_custom_pack/touhou_little_maid-1.0.0/assets/geckolib/models/entity/winefox.json

Source texture:
https://github.com/TartaricAcid/TouhouLittleMaid/blob/1.20/src/main/resources/assets/touhou_little_maid/tlm_custom_pack/touhou_little_maid-1.0.0/assets/geckolib/textures/entity/winefox.png

Changes by Blade Tetra contributors: Mikage palette and ornaments, removal
of fox features from the visible character, tailored red/white outfit,
segmented high ponytail and nape hair, skin/sock/sole shading, native rig
conversion, and new single-drawn-sword poses. Original animations are not
bundled in this rig; game poses are driven by Blade Tetra code.
