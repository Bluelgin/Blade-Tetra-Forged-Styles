# Blade Tetra: Forged Styles 1.6.5

## Qianmen · Your moves, your signature

Six moves. One very important naming decision.

- Added Qianmen custom styles. Capture ordinary moves with the Style Recorder and arrange up to six actions into your own sequence. Repeated moves are welcome.
- Name, reorder, remove, undo or clear actions in the editor, then finish a reusable native Tetra scroll.
- Choose a short, standard or long custom blade to find your preferred balance of melee reach and combo pacing.
- Import, preview, export and copy styles offline. Compact share codes preserve the name and move order, so friends can try your signature combo too.
- Includes matching recorder artwork, a paper-and-ink editor, workbench icons and recording feedback. A signature style deserves a little presentation.

## Lifeng · Custom combos, now with sword aura

- Lifeng supports custom styles across all three blade lengths, including repeated moves. Once learned, ordinary slashes emit sword aura at SSS.
- Reduced client overhead in aura-heavy fights while retaining Rengeki's crossed and Dangaku's doubled silhouettes.
- Added Main Only, Balanced and Full aura detail, plus decoration distance and count settings. Visual settings do not change damage, range or attack count.
