# Blade Tetra: Forged Styles 1.6.8

For Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge

## Named Blades and Fused Slash Arts

- Fused Slash Arts now attempt the scabbard and guard's original arts in sequence. If either art is unavailable, the other can still fire under its own conditions. Bespoke fusions keep their existing rules.
- Fixed gaps between the blade and hilt and blades sticking outside the scabbard when mixing named parts. Sheathing preserves the original scabbard where it can and can fall back to the standard scabbard when needed.
- Modular inventory backplates take their colors from the blade, scabbard, and grip while keeping the original durability border. Existing named backplates still display and can be removed at a Tetra workbench. Installing or replacing the old named backplate upgrade is no longer available.

## Forging and the Manual

- Rebalanced improvements from the three forging treatises. Damage bonuses scale with the blade's displayed attack damage, and blade construction can carry eligible material effects. Updated material costs and integrity values, and added a server-side bonus multiplier.
- With Patchouli installed, each player can receive one Bladecraft Manual on login, including in existing worlds. Modpacks can disable automatic delivery. The crafting recipe remains available.

## Fixes

- Fixed a client crash that could occur when Kyouka Suigetsu created an afterimage on teleport in some Forge installations.
- Fixed a loading issue related to Habaki knockback handling in some production client environments.
