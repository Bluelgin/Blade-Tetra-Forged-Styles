# Blade Tetra: Forged Styles 1.5.0

Named blades can now be studied. The blade may object to being homework.

- Added **Named Blade Imprinting**: place a named SlashBlade on the blade stand directly above a Tetra workbench, sneak-use a blank imprinting scroll, then complete the observe, trace and hammering trial.
- A successful study unlocks that blade's saya and complete hilt as modular fittings. Your Tetra-forged blade stays your blade—its blade body and original Slash Art are not copied.
- Supports 15 named blades from SlashBlade: Resharped and can discover standard named-blade definitions from addons; unusual models and custom renderers may still need a dedicated adapter.
- Matching saya and hilt now grant dynamic **Imprint Affinity** based on both blades: +10–30% attack and +15–35% durability, with no negative bonus when the modular blade is already stronger.
- Reworked the study screen with a proper three-stage minigame, clearer feedback, centered completion controls, optional protection/boosters and real consequences for failure.
- Reined in Iaido's overlapping combo damage, made proudsoul-ingot inscriptions awaken bewitched blades, and cleaned long tooltips into the clearer **[Alt]+ / [Alt]-** layout.
- Removed redundant generated model variants and trimmed presentation assets to keep the jar from learning Infinity Storage.

Short version: your sword can copy the drip, earn part of the performance, and keep its own soul.
