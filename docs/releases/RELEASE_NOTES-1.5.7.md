# Blade Tetra: Forged Styles 1.5.7

Prettier blades. Mikage now judges you from your inventory, too.

- Added custom Slash Arts: combine primary and secondary techniques with a Forged Slash Art Orb to create your own two-part sword skill. Soul core materials provide extra bonuses, and advanced techniques require better hammers.
- Refreshed selected item textures for a more consistent style across journals, records, clues and ritual materials. The broken mask now sports a fox-mask design.
- Improved modular blade inventory visuals with material-tinted flowing backplates, brighter blades and clearer durability frames. Awakened Akatsuki also gets a guest appearance from Mikage.
- Fixed several crashes involving summoned swords, Akatsuki abilities and Mikage's challenge.

Mikage may send you home. The server should stay where it is.
