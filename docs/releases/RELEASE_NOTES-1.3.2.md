# Blade Tetra 1.3.2

## Adventure material visual profiles

- Added hand-tuned visual profiles for Gobber, Nether Gobber, and End Gobber.
- Added dedicated dark-forged visuals for Born in Chaos dark metal.
- Added spectral and corroded profiles for EEEAB's ghost steel and immortal
  ingot materials.
- Added distinct profiles for Cataclysm ignitium, witherite, cursium, storm,
  abyssal, black steel, and ancient metal.
- Matching slash colors and particles now follow each curated material family.

All integrations match Tetra material keys at runtime. None of the source mods
or More Material Tetra are required dependencies, and no third-party textures
are copied into Blade Tetra.
