# Blade Tetra: Forged Styles 1.5.8

Mikage has left a spark of divine fire. The next swing is still yours.

- Reworked Mikage's assistance in Hundred Ghosts and higher Divine Domain trials. Instead of fighting alongside you continuously, she now sends a ring of divine fire when your health falls to 30% or below. It heals you and holds approaching ritual enemies at bay for 6 seconds, once per player per trial. It cannot revive you or protect against ranged attacks and ground hazards.
- Existing trial difficulties, waves and rewards remain. Your first successful clear now grants Beyond the Domain, closing this chapter while leaving a hint of the story ahead.
- Updated the pixel-art textures for every Karmic Mirror state, the Dead Thought Soul Seal and Sword Ghost Remnant, making their appearances easier to distinguish.
- Added workbench readouts explaining the values and activation conditions of selected blade-component effects, and supplied missing holosphere title translations for the Modular Slashblade and related items.
- Simplified Mikage's visitor dialogue screen by removing the default portrait display, and reduced the mod's download size.

For multiplayer, update both clients and the server to 1.5.8.
