# Blade Tetra: Forged Styles 1.4.1

Mikage learned a few new tricks. Unfortunately, none of them involve going easier on you.

- Overhauled Mikage's combat effects, parries, phase changes and major attack presentation.
- Rebuilt Boundary Flash with a longer airborne charge, converging arena energy, rotating torii blades and a much heavier finishing strike.
- Boundary scars are now dense, layered crimson flame walls instead of mysterious invisible geometry. Please do not stand in the fire.
- Reworked the long-range scissor attack and updated its battle hints.
- Added three Tetra-native forging guide scrolls for blade finishing, body forging and fittings.
- Kept essential blade information visible while moving detailed style explanations behind **Alt**.
- Fixed several boundary collision traps and improved multiplayer challenge cleanup and arena-slot reuse.

Short version: fewer mystery walls, more fire.
